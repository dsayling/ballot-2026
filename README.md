# Ballot 2026

A guide to the Colorado general election ballot — November 3, 2026.

All 35 items: 17 measures, 17 candidate races, and judge retentions, with
plain summaries, what a Yes and a No vote each do, fiscal notes, the case
for and against, candidate side-by-sides, and links to primary sources.

**Live site:** https://dsayling.github.io/ballot-2026/

## Your picks stay on your device

Pick an option and a confidence level for each item — only "Sure" counts
as decided. Your choices are saved only in your own browser (localStorage)
on the device you use. Nothing is sent anywhere, there are no accounts,
and nobody else can see your picks. "Copy my picks" at the bottom turns
your choices into a plain-text list you can share yourself, and
"Clear all my choices" wipes them.

The site is a single static page in `docs/index.html`, published from the
`gh-pages` branch.
